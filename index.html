All-in cash ("last mile") = price + freight/PDI + fees − incentives, plus HST, plus plates, plus the round-trip drive to collect it. It is what leaves your account to own the car outright today.
2-yr TRUE cost = what two years of driving actually costs, and the only number that compares finance to lease. On finance: down payment + payments + what you still owe, minus what the car sells for. On a lease: down payment + payments — you hand it back and own nothing, so there is no resale. Whichever is lower is the route named under the figure.
Depreciation stress test adds percentage points to every car's assumed annual depreciation. These rates are segment estimates, not queried valuations. Use it on the RAV4s: they hold ~97% of as-new price after four years, which is abnormal post-2021-shortage pricing. Push the slider to +3 or +4 and see whether they still win.
Buyback = residual × MSRP, plus HST and a ~$300 purchase-option fee. Residuals marked est are estimates and must be confirmed by the dealer; quoted came off a real quote.
