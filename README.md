# Car Deal Scout

Interactive vehicle-shopping calculator for the Greater Toronto Area — new, demo,
used and lease-takeover inventory within 200 km, with true all-in cost rather than
advertised payments.

**Live:** https://kheraashish.github.io/car-deal-scout/

## What it does

Ranks real listings by **cost to own at 24 months** — down payment + payments +
what you still owe (loan balance on finance, buyback on a lease). That is the only
apples-to-apples number across finance and lease; a low biweekly payment with a
punitive buyback is not a good deal.

Sliders recompute everything live: down payment, finance term, lease term, travel
cost per km, plates, HST.

## Why the numbers are trustworthy

The payment engine is validated against two **real Ontario dealer quotes**, and
reproduces both to the cent. Open the browser console — the self-tests run on load:

| Check | Expected | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Finance biweekly | `$374.43` | Honda quote, $43,249.72 @ 4.79% / 60 mo |
| Lease biweekly, pre-tax | `$306.24` | Toyota quote, cap $51,078.50, MSRP $59,336, 70% residual @ 6.89% / 24 mo |
| Link integrity | every deal deep-links to **one vehicle** | no search or results pages |

Break the maths, or ship a deal whose link points at a search page, and the
self-test fails loudly in the console.

### The formulas

Ontario, HST 13%.

- **Finance** — the down payment applies **after** tax, matching Ontario dealer
  worksheets: `financed = (price + fees − incentives) × 1.13 − down`, then standard
  amortization at `r = APR/26`, `n = months/12 × 26`.
- **Lease** — a cap-cost reduction applies **pre-tax**; HST rides on the payment.
  `cap = price + fees − incentives − down`, `residual = MSRP × residual%`,
  `monthly = ((cap − residual)/n + (cap + residual) × APR/2400) × 1.13`,
  `biweekly = monthly × 12/26`.
- **Buyback** = `residual × 1.13 + ~$300 purchase-option fee`.

The residual is quoted on **MSRP**, never on the selling price. Getting that
backwards understates the buyback by thousands.

- **Lease takeovers** inherit a payment already fixed by the original contract, so
  it is never recomputed — the real number is stored and the term is marked inherited.

## Hidden costs

Every deal expands to a hidden-cost table — the money that is real but never on the
advertised payment. Green = negotiable, amber = risk-only.

The ones most often missed:

- **Lease-end disposition fee, $350–450.** Charged when you hand the car back;
  waived only if you buy out or re-lease.
- **Dealer add-ons** — admin, anti-theft, nitrogen, fuel surcharge. Several hundred
  to $1,500 of pure margin, all negotiable.
- **A lease down payment is not refunded on a write-off.** Insurance pays the
  lessor; without GAP the cash is gone.
- **Advertised biweekly that excludes tax** understates by ~13%.
- **On any takeover, check remaining free-km-per-year _before_ the payment.** An
  inherited allowance below ~16,000 km/yr is a debt, not a deal.

## Caveat

Prices, kilometres, colour and distance are read from live listings. Where a rate
or residual was not confirmed by the dealer it is labelled `est` in the table and
must be confirmed before signing.

**Inventory moves fast.** A 2025 RAV4 Hybrid XLE at $43,900 was in the first sweep
and sold before the second — it has been dropped rather than left as a dead link.
Re-run the scan before acting on anything here.
